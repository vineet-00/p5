VectorForge

Evolutionary Rocket Lab

A small laboratory where tiny rockets learn to fly. Each rocket carries a sequence of force-vectors (its “DNA”). We launch a whole population, measure who flew closest to the glowing target, keep the best, mix their DNA, add a little random mutation, and try again. Over many tries, the population improves — a simple, beautiful kind of learning by natural selection.

In the words of Richard Feynman (if he liked rockets and code):
“You don’t need to understand everything at once. Try things. See what happens. Keep the ones that work and use bits of them to make better ones. That’s how these rockets learn.”
