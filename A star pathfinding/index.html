What's happening?

Imagine you're trying to walk across a field with rocks scattered about. A* behaves like a curious explorer: it keeps a list of places it could try next (open set) and a list of places it's already tried (closed set). For each new step it guesses how good that step looks now and how close it seems to the goal — add those guesses together and pick the most promising place. Repeat until you reach the goal or run out of sensible choices.

That's it. No magic — just careful bookkeeping + an honest intuition about which directions look promising.
