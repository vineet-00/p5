Harmonic Superform

A study in geometric harmonics — a 3D form generated from the Superformula. Move your mouse to steer the rotation and pace. The form and shading are intentionally muted and contemplative: quiet indigo depths with warm sand highlights.

Note: Inspired by the Superformula (J. Gielis) used to generate complex organic shapes.
