Random Walker

“The pleasure of finding things out.” — a small walker, a simple rule, and the quiet emergence of pattern.

In this sketch a point starts in the middle and takes small random steps. Every so often it makes a rare, long leap. No plan, no memory — just local choices. Over time the path becomes a delicate drawing that hints at order inside randomness.

The rules are intentionally minimal; the artwork comes from repetition and chance — something Richard Feynman celebrated: one can learn a lot from letting a system run and noticing what appears.

A simple random walk: short steps with rare long jumps. Watch the path form.
