Interactive Bubble Constellation

This dynamic digital artwork simulates a floating constellation of bubbles drifting and interacting within the canvas. Bubbles glow brightly when overlapping, symbolizing connections and sparks of interaction.

The gentle organic motion invites meditation, while user interaction allows dragging bubbles to rearrange and create unique patterns.

This blend of randomness, geometry, and interactivity creates a contemplative and playful visual experience.

Click and drag bubbles with mouse or finger to rearrange the constellation.

Bubbles glow when touching, emphasizing connection and unity.
