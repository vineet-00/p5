Asteroids: 0

What's happening?

The world is simple: rocks drift, a little ship nudges and shoots, and when a rock is hit it splits into smaller rocks. No single part is clever — the magic comes from many small interactions. That’s the charm: simple laws produce lively behavior.

Controls are intentionally minimal — thrust to move, rotate to aim, space to fire. Watch how the pieces move and how local collisions create the large-scale dance.

Controls

Keys: ← → rotate, ↑ thrust, SPACE shoot. Try the trails for a nice retro glow.
