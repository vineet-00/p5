Star Pattern

A little geometry that makes surprising stars

Hankin Lines

Imagine you're standing at the middle of each edge of every square tile. From that midpoint you shine two little rays, each tipped inward at an angle. Where these rays meet — if they meet just right — a star is sketched. The surprise is that a very simple rule applied over and over produces a rich, delicate pattern. The code below simply finds those ray intersections and draws them. That's all — but that all is beautiful.
