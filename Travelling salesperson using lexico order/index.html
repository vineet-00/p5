✦ Travelling Salesperson (lexicographic)

Let me say it plainly: you have a handful of cities and you want the shortest route that visits each one exactly once. One brute force way is to list every possible order of cities, measure the length of each, and remember the shortest — tedious but certain. The trick here is not to be clever about shortcuts but to walk through the permutations in a neat, lexicographic order so we don't miss anything. Count, compare, keep the best — that's it. It's simple, obvious, and honest.

This demo steps through permutations in lexicographic order. The top-left path (magenta) is the best-so-far. The lower area shows the current permutation under test. The page is laid out so the explanation and canvas are visible together without scrolling.
