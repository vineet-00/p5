✦ TF–IDF Explorer

Let me tell you plainly: TF–IDF is a way to ask a simple question about words — “Which words are important in this one document compared to the rest?”

First we count how often a word appears in our document (that’s the “term frequency”). Then we ask — is that word rare across the other documents? If it is, that rarity makes it more interesting. Multiply frequency by a measure of rarity and you get TF–IDF: a little number that captures “this word matters here.” That’s it. No magic — just counting and weighing, and the numbers do the talking.

This page loads a few sample text files, computes TF (on the first file), computes document frequency (DF) across all files, then computes TF–IDF and lists words sorted by TF–IDF score.
