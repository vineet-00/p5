Phyllotaxis Pattern

Phyllotaxis is the mathematical study of leaf arrangement in plants, revealing one of nature's most elegant patterns based on the golden angle (137.5°).

This visualization demonstrates how plants like sunflowers, pinecones, and succulents arrange their seeds or leaves using this optimal angle to maximize space efficiency and light exposure.

Each point is positioned using polar coordinates where the angle increases by the golden angle and the radius grows proportionally to the square root of the point number, creating the characteristic spiral patterns found throughout nature.

The colors shift dynamically based on both angle and radius, creating a mesmerizing display that combines mathematical precision with natural beauty.

The pattern evolves continuously, revealing the underlying mathematical harmony of natural growth patterns.
