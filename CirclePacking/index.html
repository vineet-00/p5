Circle Packing

Watch circles grow and settle — maximal packing born from gentle rules.

What's happening?

Think of each circle as a polite kid on a playground who wants to stretch out but won't push others. Each starts tiny and grows slowly until it bumps another circle or the edge — then it stops. The result is a quiet packing: no overlaps, just many little local decisions that produce a beautiful global pattern.

In short: many simple rules + gentle growth → complex, pleasing packing.
