Markov Text Generator

A Markov Generator creates new text by analyzing patterns in existing text sequences.

Imagine reading a book and noticing how certain letters tend to follow others—like "q" is almost always followed by "u." That’s what a Markov chain does: it learns the rules of sequence from a given text. Then, instead of copying it word for word, it plays a statistical guessing game—choosing the most likely next character based on what came before. The result? New passages that echo the style of the source but wander into surprising, sometimes nonsensical, sometimes poetic territory.
