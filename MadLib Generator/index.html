MadLibs Generator

MadLibs is a creative word game where blanks in a story template are filled with random words to generate silly, fun stories. This project loads a word list from CSV and replaces placeholders dynamically to produce unique stories on each generation.

"______! ($$Exclamation$$!) they said ______ ($$Adverb$$) as they jumped into their ______ ($$Noun$$) and flew off with their ______ ($$Adjective$$) ______ ($$PluralNoun$$)."
