Particles Flux

Attraction & Repulsion — a tiny lab for intuition

Here each particle is a little explorer and each green point is an invisible teacher. Particles are nudged by simple inverse-square forces: sometimes they pull, sometimes (when they get very close) they push away. Watch patterns emerge from these playful rules — it’s the same spirit Feynman used for building intuition: small rules, surprising behaviour.
