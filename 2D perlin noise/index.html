2D Perlin Noise

Explore smooth, organic textures generated from noise.

Brightness: Low/High values

What's happening?

2D Perlin noise is like a calm, unpredictable landscape. Each pixel’s brightness is determined by gently averaging “opinions” from neighboring points in two dimensions. The result is smooth, natural-looking patterns — no harsh jumps, just soft gradients.

Dark areas correspond to low values, light areas to high values. If you animate it, the patterns seem alive, slowly flowing and evolving, like clouds drifting in the sky.

In one sentence: 2D Perlin noise creates smooth, organic textures with a natural flow.
