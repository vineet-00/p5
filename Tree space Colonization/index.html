✦ Tree — Space Colonization

I'll put it simply: the tree doesn't decide where to grow by guessing — it responds to food. The leaves are little targets floating around the canopy. Branch tips sense the nearby leaves; each leaf nudges the nearest tip toward it. Tips that gather many nudges grow; leaves that are reached disappear. Step after step the branches push outward and the canopy fills itself — that's all there is. Count, nudge, grow, repeat. Nothing mystical, just a tidy rule that makes a tree.

This sketch implements the Space Colonization algorithm: a cloud of leaves attract branch tips; tips grow toward the averaged attraction and new branches are spawned. The algorithm parameters are the same as your original code.
