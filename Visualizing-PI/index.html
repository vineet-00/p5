Pi — A Feynman Sketch

Turning digits into ink-strokes. A playful, compact visualization inspired by comic-book layout and the curious voice of Richard Feynman.

Why this works

“If you want to know a little about nature, you should not be afraid of numbers. Here I try to see the spirit of π by letting its digits draw.”

This sketch maps each digit (0–9) to a point on a circle, then draws lines between successive digits. Patterns emerge when many digits meet — like tiny footprints of the number.
